# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on GitHub, as confirmed by Alexander.

## Users

Alexander, choosing a place to play golf or practise at a driving range with friends.

## Product Purpose

A personal index of courses and ranges that makes comparing options and opening the right booking or availability page quick.

## Operating Context

The user referenced SF Court Weekend as the model for a page-based local index. The page should collect prices, booking or availability links, locations, and practical details. It will use GitHub.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Show the difference between a course, a range, and a venue offering both.
- Link to the venue's own availability and pricing pages.
- Treat published prices as dated snapshots; live and variable fees must stay visibly variable.
- Geographic scope: San Francisco, Marin, Berkeley, Richmond, Oakland, Alameda, Walnut Creek and roughly halfway down the East Bay; south along the Peninsula through Palo Alto and Sunnyvale. Exclude San Jose.
- No claim of live availability from third-party booking systems.

## Evidence on Hand

- The existing local `output/sf-court-weekend` project provides the requested index precedent.
- Official venue pages provide the initial rates, addresses, and booking links.

## Product Principles

- Reach a booking page in one action.
- Make fixed and variable prices easy to distinguish.
- Keep every fact traceable to its venue source.
