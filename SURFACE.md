# Golf register

## Scope and mode

Single-page, task-focused index. Visitor mode: Operate.

## Job

Find a public place in the broader Bay Area to play nine or eighteen holes or use a driving range, compare published prices, and open the venue's booking page.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A pocket yardage book for choosing the next outing: the useful information sits in a clear ruled register, not in a promotional grid.

**OWN-WORLD:** Cool field paper, deep evergreen ink, pale grass green, and small coral marks. Fine horizontal rules and compact tabular figures make prices easy to scan. The serif title gives the page a personal field-guide voice; controls use a plain sans face.

**STORY:** Choose play or practice, narrow the geography and round length, compare the visible fees, then follow a direct venue link. Source and review dates are visible at the point of comparison.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A large title and short explanation sit above a full-width filter strip. A slim summary line leads into a dense list; the first rows show venue, city, course length, standard fee, range price, and a booking action without scrolling sideways.

**FORM:** A field-book register with responsive rows. This was grounded candidate five in the Impeccable direction roll (seed 4a96cd5d). The signature interaction is a fast, in-place reordering of the same rows as filters change.
