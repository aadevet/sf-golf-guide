Before you book

Fees are published snapshots, not live quotes. Some courses change prices by demand, residency, time of day, or cart choice. Use Check times for availability and the linked rate page for the exact terms. Driving ranges are usually walk-up; check the venue’s hours before travelling. I could not verify public booking at a Richmond course; Tilden Park in Berkeley and Franklin Canyon in Hercules are nearby options.
